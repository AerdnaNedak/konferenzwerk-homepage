Your benefit

What do you gain as the client?

Why work with an external conference designer?

You avoid costly detours in content. An independent view connects company goals, audience and dramaturgy – so contributions fit, do not repeat each other and add up to a coherent conference day.

How do many topics become a coherent programme?

You get a clear line instead of a collection of topics. A central idea gives topics, speakers, sequence and formats a shared yardstick.

What do you actually gain during the project?

You are relieved of content and organisational work. Decisions are well-founded, responsibilities are clear and speakers are reliably managed. On the day itself, you have room to be the host.

How does the conference make an impact beyond the day?

Impact is part of the plan from the very start. Tools, exchange, contacts, next steps and digital follow-up carry new ideas into everyday work.

Andrea Kaden · Founder of KonferenzWerk · Conference designer

I see the whole day – and the people in it.

I have experienced countless conferences: the inspiring ones, the surprising ones and the ones where a good topic fizzled out on stage.

Today I combine this experience with careful work on the audience. I know when an audience needs a new thought, when it needs participation, when a break – and which speaker can truly tune in to the people in the room.

Since 2020 I have been designing audience-specific in-house conferences myself – eight formats so far with 50 to 300 participants, four of which I moderated myself. Conference design and collaboration are possible in German or English; on stage, I moderate in German.

KonferenzWerk is my framework for this: clear in responsibility, personal in collaboration and always focused on what should remain after the last item on the programme.

Alongside this, I run WORK WITH EASE together with Ralf Westphal. There we help people in the office work more simply, more digitally and with AI.

If you would like to see me on stage yourself – as a moderator or with a talk on AI in the world of work – you will find my profile on Speakerinnen.org. My career path is on LinkedIn.

In brief

Frequently asked questions

Can I book just one service area?

Yes. Concept, speaker management, moderation and the digital participant journey can each be booked separately. In our first conversation we clarify what is already in place and where my work makes the biggest difference.

Do you also take on speaker sourcing only?

Yes. I take care of research, selection, outreach and briefing. If contributions do not suit the audience, repeat each other or leave a gap in content, I say so openly.

Which audiences do you work for?

For a wide range of internal specialist and professional groups. What matters is not the industry but that a specific audience is to be developed, connected or strengthened in times of change through an in-person conference.

What does working together cost?

The price depends on your starting point, the scope of services and the timeframe. After our first conversation you receive a tailored proposal instead of an ill-fitting standard package.
