In brief

Frequently asked questions

Is KonferenzWerk an event agency?

No. KonferenzWerk is content-led event management: my focus is on content design, the people and the impact of the conference. Venue and catering remain with you or your event partners.

Do we have to commission the entire conference design?

No. You can commission individual building blocks or overall responsibility for content, speakers, participant journey and delivery. In our first conversation we clarify what is already well covered internally.

What does participant management include?

Depending on your needs, you receive a conference website or app in your corporate identity with registration, track selection, a watch list, a personal view, rebooking, cancellation, waiting lists and feedback. All information comes together in one place – instead of in Excel lists, Forms surveys and multiple file versions. This saves time and gives you clarity at any moment.

Do you also handle delivery on site?

Yes. If you wish, I moderate the conference and take over the operational management of the programme, the speakers and the technology partners involved on site.

Do you take responsibility for budgets and technology?

If needed, I take care of selecting and managing the technology as well as budget responsibility for speakers and technology. The exact framework is agreed transparently before the project starts.

Do we need to introduce new software to work together?

No. I adapt to the tools you already use – for example Microsoft 365 with Copilot, Google Workspace or your project management system. Where it helps, we structure tasks and decisions with Kanban. That way everyone involved can see the current status from anywhere. This saves uncertainty, file versions and follow-up questions.
